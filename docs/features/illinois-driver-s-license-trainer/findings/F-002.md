## Finding

DEC-002 rejected the gamified progression (Q-002 C) and states 'Gamification elements can be layered on later'. REQ-004's acceptance criterion makes streaks/badges 'or similar' a v1 requirement. An implementer cannot tell whether v1 needs a persistent achievement system (badges, streak tracking in local storage) or just celebratory copy/animation on completion.

> Progress and achievements are celebrated (e.g. streaks, badges or similar).

## Possible interpretations

- v1 only needs celebratory feedback messages/animations on topic completion and passed simulation — no persistent achievements
- v1 includes a minimal achievement system (badges/streaks) stored locally alongside progress

## Resolution

AI: В v1 — только праздничная обратная связь (сообщения/анимации при завершении темы, достижении порога и сдаче симуляции), без постоянной системы бейджей и серий. Формулировку критерия REQ-004 уточняем: «Progress milestones (topic completion, readiness, passed simulation) are celebrated with encouraging feedback; a persistent achievement system (badges, streaks) is out of v1 scope» — это согласуется с DEC-002.
