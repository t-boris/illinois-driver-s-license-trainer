## Statement

The application offers a Flashcards mode for signs and for glossary terms: a card shows a sign (or a term), the user taps to reveal its name and meaning (or definition), and moves to the next card. It is a study aid only: it does not affect readiness and stores no progress, badges or streaks.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The Signs section offers a Flashcards mode that shows each official sign image and reveals its name and meaning in the active language on tap.
- [ ] The Terms section offers a Flashcards mode that shows the English term and reveals the Russian equivalent and definition on tap.
- [ ] Cards can be advanced forward and back and shuffled; the mode works with keyboard and touch.
- [ ] No progress, score or streak is stored or shown; readiness is unaffected.
- [ ] Card flip and slide animations are disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
