## Statement

The application must tell users how to schedule the Illinois written/driver's license exam and link to the official Illinois Secretary of State scheduling and preparation pages. The app itself does not book appointments (DEC-020).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A "Book your exam" page is reachable from the home screen in both Russian and English with identical content.
- [ ] The page gives step-by-step instructions: schedule on the official scheduler (up to 90 days in advance), bring acceptable identification documents, find a facility, cancel or modify an existing appointment.
- [ ] The page links to the official SOS Appointments page, DMV scheduler, cancel/modify, facility finder, acceptable-documents checklist and REAL ID information; links open in a new tab with rel="noopener noreferrer".
- [ ] The page mentions the seniors-only phone line and that oral exams for the written test can be requested in person Monday to Thursday.
- [ ] The page states that scheduling happens on the official site and the app does not book or store appointments.
