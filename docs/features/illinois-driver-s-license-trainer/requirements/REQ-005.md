## Statement

Learning content must be heavily illustrated. Decorative and scene imagery (scenes, characters, decoration) may be AI-generated, but every road sign and traffic signal shown for learning must be taken from the official FHWA MUTCD / Standard Highway Signs (SHS) files (US federal government works, public domain — external fact to be confirmed and recorded), supplemented by official Illinois-specific sources where a sign is state-specific. AI-generated imagery is never used for the sign or signal itself. The source of each sign asset is recorded in content metadata. A sign asset library is assembled from the official files during content preparation; AI-generated scene images that contain signs must either composite the official sign asset or avoid depicting signs as the subject.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every topic/lesson and the majority of questions have an accompanying image.
- [ ] Every road sign or traffic signal image shown for learning (lessons, practice, exam simulation) is sourced from the official FHWA MUTCD / SHS files or, for state-specific signs, from an official Illinois source; no AI-generated sign or signal images are present.
- [ ] Road signs used in sign questions match the official MUTCD/Illinois appearance (shape, colour, symbol, text).
- [ ] Each sign asset has content metadata recording its source (file/publication and sign designation); no sign asset without a recorded source is shipped.
- [ ] A sign asset library built from the official files exists in the content pipeline and is the only source of sign imagery used by the app.
- [ ] Every AI-generated scene image that contains a sign either composites the official sign asset from the library or does not depict the sign as the subject of the image.
- [ ] The public-domain status of the FHWA MUTCD / SHS files is confirmed and recorded as an external fact in the project documentation.
- [ ] Images have alt text in the active language.
- [ ] Image sizes are optimised so pages load quickly on mobile networks.
