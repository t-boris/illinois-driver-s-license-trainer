## Context

DEC-008 requires RU and EN review records (reviewer, date) per content item and fails the build without them. The assistant can draft but cannot attest a human review.

## Alternatives

1. Seed bank only
2. Stop at app + gate

## Decision

The assistant drafts lessons and questions as unreviewed pull-request content. Review records are added only by the owner or a designee after real review. Items without both records are never deployed, and the assistant never writes review records itself.

## Reason

Keeps the DEC-001 and DEC-008 guarantee honest.

## Consequences

Until reviews exist the production build fails or the bank is empty; the app is built and tested against fixtures kept outside the deploy path.
