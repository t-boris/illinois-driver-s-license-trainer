## Context

Secondary sources list English, Spanish, Chinese, Korean and Polish (interpreters allowed) and not Russian, which conflicts with the premise in F-001/DEC-007. The official ilsos.gov page returned HTTP 403 to the assistant. Secondary sources also give 35 questions (15 signs, 20 rules) and a pass mark of 28/35 (80%), unconfirmed.

## Alternatives

1. Record secondary-source list as unconfirmed and use neutral wording

## Decision

The owner checks the official SOS page and supplies the exact language list, question count, rules/signs mix and pass threshold. The assistant records them as external facts with source URL and date, and words the in-app informational note (DEC-007) from them. Until then no copy states or implies that Russian is offered.

## Reason

The official source is the only acceptable basis (DEC-007); the assistant cannot reach it.

## Consequences

I-1 is blocked on the owner's input; simulation parameters and the language note stay provisional (35 / 15 signs / 20 rules / 28 pass) until confirmed.
