## Context

The Illinois written test questions are not officially published. The team had to choose between authoring questions from the official manual, generating them with AI without systematic verification, or licensing an existing bank.

## Alternatives

1. B — AI-generated question bank without systematic verification against the manual
2. C — Reuse and translate an existing open/licensed question bank

## Decision

All lessons, quiz questions, answers and explanations are written (manually or with AI drafts) strictly on the basis of the official Illinois Rules of the Road and are manually reviewed in both languages before publication. Every question is traceable to a section of the manual.

## Reason

The product goal is maximum exam readiness, so factual accuracy outweighs speed. AI may draft, but a human verifies against the official source.

## Consequences

Content production is the main effort of v1; a review step and a source reference per question are required. Content must be re-checked when the manual is updated.
