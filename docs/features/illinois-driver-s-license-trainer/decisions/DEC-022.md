## Context

DEC-002 defines the path lesson → quiz → exam simulation. The owner expects the app to also work as a study tool with separate areas rather than only tests, and names signs and terms explicitly.

## Alternatives

1. Keep only the lesson → quiz → simulation path

## Decision

The home screen is a hub with separate sections: Learn (illustrated lessons per topic), Signs (a browsable gallery of road signs, signals and markings grouped by category, each with its name and meaning, using only the official sign library per DEC-010), Terms (a bilingual RU/EN glossary of exam terms), Practice (topic quizzes), Exam (simulation), plus progress/readiness and Book your exam. Terms and sign cards are content items: they come from the official manual (DEC-001), carry a section reference, the manual version and RU/EN review records, and are gated at build time like all content (DEC-008). The lesson → quiz → simulation path and readiness rules (DEC-002) are unchanged; Signs and Terms are study aids and do not affect readiness.

## Reason

A learner needs to study, look things up and drill separately; terms support the RU→EN bridge (DEC-005, REQ-004).

## Consequences

Two new content kinds (term, signcard) in the schema and validator; navigation changes; more content to draft and review. Adds REQ-010.
