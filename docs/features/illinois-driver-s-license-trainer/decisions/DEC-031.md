## Context

DEC-013, DEC-008 and REQ-003/REQ-006 keep unreviewed content off every public URL, so the live site was empty. The owner wants content visible now and chose to publish the AI drafts with a clear label.

## Alternatives

1. The owner reviews and promotes everything first
2. Keep the site empty until review

## Decision

The deployment may publish the AI-drafted content from content-drafts/ in a beta mode that is switched on only by the build variable VITE_PUBLISH_DRAFTS=1 (set for the Vercel production environment). In beta mode: a persistent banner on every screen says in both languages that the materials are drafts that have not yet been reviewed by a reviewer against the Rules of the Road and that the official manual is the authority; every lesson and every question carries a "Draft" tag; drafts are still fact-checked against the manual and must pass `validate-content.mjs content-drafts --draft` (structure, bilingual text, sign library references) or the build fails. Reviewed content in content/ keeps the full gate (section reference, manual version, RU/EN review records) and is shown without any draft tag. This amends DEC-013, DEC-008 and the "never deployed" wording of REQ-003 and REQ-006 for drafts only; the review records remain the proof of human review, and the assistant still never writes them. When an item is promoted to content/, the label disappears. Turning VITE_PUBLISH_DRAFTS off restores the strict behaviour.

## Reason

The owner prefers visible, clearly labelled beta material over an empty site; the label keeps the learner informed about its status.

## Consequences

Learners may see content that has not been reviewed by a human; the label and the manual-first fact-check mitigate this. REQ-003 and REQ-006 acceptance criteria about unreviewed content apply to content/ only, and to drafts only when the beta flag is off.
