## Context

REQ-005 requires accurate signs but allows AI illustrations; AI generators do not reproduce exact sign geometry and text, and no source for sign images was named.

## Alternatives

1. Use public-domain FHWA MUTCD / Standard Highway Signs drawings
2. Attempt to AI-generate signs and manually correct them
3. Hand-draw signs in-house

## Decision

All road signs and traffic signals shown for learning come from the official FHWA MUTCD / Standard Highway Signs files (US federal government works, public domain — to be confirmed and recorded as an external fact), supplemented by Illinois-specific official sources where a sign is state-specific. AI-generated imagery is used only for scenes, characters and decoration, never for the sign itself. The source of each sign asset is recorded in content metadata.

## Reason

Guarantees exact official appearance required by REQ-005 at zero licensing cost and stops the content team from spending effort on generating signs.

## Consequences

A sign asset library is assembled from the official files during content prep; AI-generated scene images that contain signs must either composite the official sign or avoid depicting signs as the subject.
