## Context

The bilingual requirement could serve several audiences; the team had to pick who v1 is optimised for.

## Alternatives

1. B — teenagers 15–18 preparing for the first exam
2. C — everyone taking the Illinois test, Russian as a secondary language

## Decision

v1 is designed for Russian-speaking adults in Illinois taking the written test for the first time or re-qualifying. English remains fully supported, but tone, examples and explanations are written with this audience in mind.

## Reason

Bilingualism is the distinctive value of the product and matters most to this group.

## Consequences

Adult-oriented humour and examples; no special handling for minors' data. Russian explanations should bridge to English terminology used on the real exam (how exactly — see open question).
