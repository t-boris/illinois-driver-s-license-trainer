## Context

Chrome showed a Safe Browsing "Dangerous site" warning for illinois-license-trainer.vercel.app. Google's public Safe Browsing report lists no flags for the domain; the warning is consistent with a heuristic false positive for a new vercel.app address with government-themed content. The app is not an official Illinois Secretary of State product.

## Alternatives

1. Do nothing and wait for the flag to clear

## Decision

Every page footer states in both languages that the app is an unofficial study aid not affiliated with the Illinois Secretary of State and that the official source is the Illinois Rules of the Road at ilsos.gov. The page has a meta description. The owner reports the false positive to Google (Safe Browsing report) and may move the app to a custom domain, which requires a purchase and the owner's approval.

## Reason

Clear, honest provenance helps users and reduces impersonation-style signals; it is also true and consistent with DEC-001.

## Consequences

One extra footer line in both languages; no change to features or data.
