## Context

There are no accounts (DEC-004), so analytics is the only way to learn which topics and questions are weak or badly worded — essential for the goal of maximum exam readiness.

## Alternatives

1. A: no analytics in v1

## Decision

v1 collects anonymous, aggregated usage analytics (page/topic views, per-question and per-topic correct/incorrect counts, simulation pass/fail rates) without identifying the user, without cookies or persistent identifiers that would require a consent banner. A short privacy note in the app describes what is collected.

## Reason

Exposes weak content without collecting personal data; achievable without a consent banner when implemented correctly.

## Consequences

A privacy note must be written in both languages. Analytics events must be designed to carry no personal data or stable user identifiers. Content team gains data to improve weak questions.
