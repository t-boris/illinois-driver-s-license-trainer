## Context

The 2026 manual (DSD A 112.47) describes the exam as signs, signals, pavement markings and questions on traffic laws, safety rules, crash prevention and vehicle equipment, but does not list the examined chapters (SRC-002).

## Alternatives

1. Chapters 4-7, 9-12 only (proposal)
2. Owner-specified list

## Decision

v1 topics are derived from chapters 3 (Drivers Under Age 21), 4 (Traffic Laws), 5 (Sharing the Road), 6 (DUI), 7 (Traffic Violations/Crashes), 8 (License Revocation, Suspension, Denial and Cancellation), 9 (Roadway Signs), 10 (Traffic Signals and Pavement Markings), 11 (Safe Driving Tips), 12 (Equipment For Safe Driving). Chapters 1, 2 and 13 are excluded as administrative. Chapter 3 copy stays adult-oriented and is written for adults, per DEC-003 and REQ-004.

## Reason

Owner's scoping choice; covers all rule-bearing chapters.

## Consequences

Ten topics; per DEC-009 the question bank per topic is sized from the 35-question composition (15 signs, 20 rules). Signs (chapter 9) carries the 15-question sign share; the 20 rules questions are shared across the other topics. Supersedes implementation/topics-proposal.md.
