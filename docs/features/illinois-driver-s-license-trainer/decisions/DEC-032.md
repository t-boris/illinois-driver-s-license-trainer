## Context

DEC-009 requires full coverage of the examined chapters and REQ-005 requires official sign imagery. The first content pass covered the main points of each chapter and 29 signs. The owner asked for a check that everything needed is present and for a livelier site.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the current coverage and visuals

## Decision

(1) Coverage audit. Every examined chapter (DEC-016) is compared with the manual page by page. Facts a learner may be tested on that are missing from a lesson are added; missing questions and glossary terms are drafted; every sign the manual shows in chapter 9 and the sign names listed in the official workbook's sign-identification section are added to the Signs gallery with the official FHWA drawing; where the official files provide signal and marking artwork it is added too, and anything without official artwork is reported rather than drawn by AI. New and changed content is fact-checked against the manual again and stays a labelled draft (DEC-031). (2) Dynamic interface. Page transitions, animated progress and hero, answer and celebration animations, hover and press feedback, a reading-progress bar in lessons, and a Flashcards mode for signs and terms (tap to reveal, next, no scoring, nothing stored). Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion. Persistent achievements (badges, streaks, trophies) stay out of v1 (F-002, REQ-004).

## Reason

The product promise is full exam preparation (DEC-009); a livelier interface supports the playful tone (REQ-004).

## Consequences

More drafts to review; new REQ-011 for flashcards. Flashcards do not change readiness and store no progress.
