## Context

After the coverage audit (DEC-032) a topic can have up to 67 quiz questions (traffic laws) and 125 rule questions are in the exam pool. One quiz session over a whole topic would be far too long, and the earlier threshold (5 answers at 80%) is too weak for such banks. DEC-002 leaves the per-topic threshold to the implementer.

## Alternatives

1. Ask every question of the topic in one session

## Decision

A topic quiz session asks 10 questions drawn from that topic's quiz pool, unanswered or previously missed questions first, then questions already answered correctly, shuffled within each group; the questions of a session are fixed for its duration. A topic counts as complete when at least 10 distinct questions of its quiz pool (fewer if the pool is smaller) have been answered and at least 80% of those answers are correct (last answer per question). The exam simulation is unchanged (15 sign + 20 rule questions from the exam pool) and the readiness rule of DEC-002 is unchanged.

## Reason

Short sessions suit phone use; preferring unseen and missed questions makes repeated sessions cover the whole bank; the higher threshold makes "topic complete" more meaningful.

## Consequences

Constants QUIZ_LENGTH and TOPIC_THRESHOLD in src/lib/config.ts. Learners reach topic completion after a first session of 10 with at least 8 correct.
