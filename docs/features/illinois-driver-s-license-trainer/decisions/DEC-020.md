## Context

F-006 confirmed "DMV appointment booking" as out of v1 scope. The owner now asks the app to include links and instructions for scheduling the exam.

## Alternatives

1. Build in-app appointment booking (rejected: out of scope per F-006, no access to the SOS booking system)

## Decision

The app has a bilingual "Book your exam" page with step-by-step instructions and links to the official Illinois Secretary of State pages: the Appointments page, the DMV appointment scheduler, cancel/modify, the facility finder, the acceptable-documents checklist, REAL ID information, and the seniors phone line. It also states that oral exams for the written test can be requested in person Monday through Thursday (manual p. 15). The app does not book, store or process any appointment data. F-006's OUT list is unchanged: in-app booking stays out of v1.

## Reason

Helps users take the next real-world step without adding booking functionality or personal data.

## Consequences

One extra page and copy in both languages; external links must be re-checked when the SOS site changes. Adds REQ-009.
