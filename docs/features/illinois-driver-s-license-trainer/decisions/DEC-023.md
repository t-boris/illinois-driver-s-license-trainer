## Context

REQ-005 asks for heavily illustrated content with AI-generated scene and decorative images, but the spec does not name a generator (overview, Dependencies).

## Alternatives

1. The assistant writes prompts and the owner generates the images
2. Defer scene images past the first deployment

## Decision

The owner chooses the image generator and gives the assistant access (API key or equivalent). The assistant then builds the generation pipeline (prompts per lesson/topic, bilingual alt text, size optimisation) and generates the images. AI images are used only for scenes, characters and decoration, never for signs or signals (DEC-010). Until access is provided, REQ-005's illustration criteria stay open.

## Reason

Keeps the choice of tool and its cost with the owner.

## Consequences

REQ-005 acceptance criteria 1 (image per lesson and most questions), 6 and 9 remain open until the owner supplies the generator; official sign images already work.
