## Context

The spec does not state how many topics or questions v1 must contain, which drives the main v1 effort and the credibility of the per-topic readiness threshold.

## Alternatives

1. Full-coverage bank from day one, sized by the real test's composition
2. Minimal viable bank per topic that grows over time

## Decision

v1 covers every chapter of the official Illinois Rules of the Road that is examined on the written test, as topics (rules and road signs). The minimum bank per topic is defined functionally: enough questions to fill that topic's share of a full exam simulation and a separate topic quiz without the two repeating the same questions. The actual numbers are fixed once the real test's question count and rules/signs mix are verified (REQ-003); the bank may grow after v1.

## Reason

The product promise is 'as ready as possible for the exam', which a partial bank cannot deliver; tying size to the real exam composition avoids inventing arbitrary numbers while keeping the readiness indicator meaningful.

## Consequences

Content production for v1 is sized accordingly and becomes the main scheduling driver. Topic list is derived from the manual's table of contents during content prep. Image count for REQ-005 follows from the bank size.
